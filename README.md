## The roast

> My phone says storage full while hoarding 4,000 meme screenshots I'll never open again — my camera roll is just a museum of bad decisions.

One line, one laugh: it roasts my own phone on a specific, recognisable
detail — a camera roll stuffed with meme screenshots nobody will ever open
again — and the punchline turns on the last few words, reframing the
camera roll as a museum of bad decisions.

## How it works

Run `node main.ts` (Node 22.18+). It writes the single-line roast to
`/output/entry.txt`, asserting first that it is exactly one line of 1–200
characters.
